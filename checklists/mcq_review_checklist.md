# Medical MCQ Review Checklist

## Structure
- [ ] Question stem is complete and clear.
- [ ] Four or five options are present, depending on schema.
- [ ] Only one best answer exists.
- [ ] No overlapping correct options.
- [ ] No negative wording without clear emphasis.

## Clinical content
- [ ] The question uses acceptable medical terminology.
- [ ] The answer reflects a reasonable standard of care.
- [ ] The explanation does not contain unsupported claims.
- [ ] Units and values are correct.
- [ ] Drug names are not confused due to look-alike names.
- [ ] The question is not overly treatment-specific without context.

## Fairness / ambiguity
- [ ] The correct answer can be justified.
- [ ] Distractors are plausible but clearly less correct.
- [ ] The stem does not give away the answer.
- [ ] If multiple answers may be valid, add a flag for SME review.

## Output language
- [ ] Vietnamese explanation is clear and concise.
- [ ] Any English justification is short and evidence-based.
